Get to know us

About Khency's Beauty & Wellness Spa

A calm, welcoming space focused on beauty, relaxation and personalised care.

Our History

Khency's Beauty & Wellness Spa was created to provide a peaceful and welcoming environment where clients can relax, refresh and take care of their beauty and wellness needs. Our services bring professional treatments together with a calm, client-focused experience.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality beauty and wellness services while creating a relaxing experience where every client feels welcomed, respected and cared for.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted beauty and wellness destination known for professional service, a welcoming atmosphere and customer satisfaction.

Our Team

Professional Beauty Therapists

Our team is dedicated to providing friendly, professional and personalised services designed around the needs of every client.

Our Target Audience

Khency's Beauty & Wellness Spa is aimed at adults and young adults who value personal grooming, skincare, relaxation and wellness services.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to book an appointment?

We offer facial treatments, massage therapy, manicures, waxing and cupping therapy.
